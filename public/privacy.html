Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 02-Oct-2025

Introduction

Tuhfah Tracker respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: Name, email address, and profile picture to personalize your app experience.
  • Google Drive Files (Read-Only): Files inside the shared folder you have access to, so you can view and track study materials. We cannot edit, delete, or upload files.
  • User Progress Data: Watched status and notes that you create, stored securely in Firebase Firestore.

How We Use Your Data

  • To display your course materials from Google Drive.
  • To help you track study progress and notes.
  • To associate your data securely with your account across devices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Your information is only used within Tuhfah Tracker to provide the study-tracking features.
